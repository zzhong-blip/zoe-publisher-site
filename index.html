Zoe Publisher

A private application used only by its owner to prepare and publish the owner's original video content to the owner's own channel.

Single owner No third-party accounts OAuth 2.0 Owner-controlled visibility
YouTube API integration

Publishing component

Zoe Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload owner-created videos, read processing/status information, set thumbnails, and manage playlist membership for the owner's channel.

Privacy

Transparent data handling

The Privacy Policy explains the Google OAuth permissions used by the application, how authorized data is handled, how access can be revoked, and how deletion requests are processed.

Read the Privacy Policy

Owner control

Upload and visibility settings

The owner explicitly controls whether a YouTube upload is performed and selects the visibility setting used by the publishing workflow: public, unlisted, or private.

Service scope

No public SaaS or third-party upload service

Zoe Publisher is not offered to the public, does not provide YouTube API access to third parties, and does not authorize uploads to third-party YouTube accounts. The public pages on this site exist to identify the application and publish its privacy, terms, and compliance information.
